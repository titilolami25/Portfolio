About Me

I’m Titilola Okusolubo — an entrepreneur and product leader with a strong background in front-end development (React, JavaScript, Redux, Tailwind), data analysis (Power BI, Excel), and business strategy. Over the years, I’ve delivered high-impact solutions, trained startup founders, and built user-focused products.

My coding journey includes developing a modern e-commerce platform, delivering a gender diversity data analysis dashboard with Power BI, and building the corporate website for Pleasant Fresh using custom APIs. I thrive at the intersection of creativity and technology, helping businesses grow through clean design and solid engineering.

When I’m not working, I mentor entrepreneurs, experiment with new UI trends, and explore Afro-fusion pastries through my venture AfroPastry Fusion Ltd.
